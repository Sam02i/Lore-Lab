Step 1 · open weights

Gemma proposes

Local open-weight Gemma reads the proverb, translates it and sorts the sign into a closed vocabulary. It never judges whether the saying is true, and it never invents a threshold. Anything vague goes into an ambiguities list for you to settle.

sign.kind atmospheric | animal | plant | celestial | body proxy.variable pressure_msl | wind_direction_10m | ...| none proxy.fit good | rough | none threshold_suggestion null // always. You set the number.
Step 2 · plain Python

Code decides

A small model should not be the judge of what can be tested. Backtestability is computed by deterministic code and stored on the hypothesis.

backtestable = sign.kind == "atmospheric" and proxy.variable in WHITELIST and proxy.fit != "none"

A "rough" fit is labelled Rough match and needs your explicit OK.

Two ways to test

Lab Test and Field Test

Lab Test

The sign shows up in weather data. We score it against about 10 years of Open-Meteo history for your spot. Big sample, instant answer, modelled reanalysis rather than station readings.

pressure changewind directionhigh cloudhumidity

Field Test

Swifts, cows, crickets, the look of the moon: no instrument records them, so you are the instrument. Look, tap Seen or Not seen, put your phone away. Small sample, and the app says so.

animalsplantssoundsthe moon
Step 4 · outcomes

Lazy outcome check

No scheduler, no queue. Each look stores a time to resolve after. Whenever the app next opens, one idempotent function fills in what the sky actually did.

resolvePending() find looks that are due claim the row (no double-resolve) fetch hourly rain for the window missing hour? retry, up to 5 times save outcome, update scorecard
Step 5 · the scorecard

Never a verdict the data can't back

Every card shows the bar to beat (how often it rains anyway) next to the hit rate, with sample size beside every number. Under the minimum, you get a notebook page of blank tally dots instead of a percentage.

House rules

Honest by design

Backtest and field results are shown separately and never pooled. One place and a short history is a hint, not proof. No streaks, badges or leaderboards, because the reward is the open question.
