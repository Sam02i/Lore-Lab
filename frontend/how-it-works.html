    Open weights

    Gemma proposes.

    Local Gemma translates the proverb and sorts the sign into a closed vocabulary, without judging it or inventing a threshold.

threshold_suggestion  null  // always. You set the number.

    Two ways to test

    Test it.

    Lab Test

    Scored against about 10 years of Open-Meteo history for your spot.

    Field Test

    No instrument records swifts or cows, so you are the instrument.

    Outcomes resolve whenever the app next opens. No scheduler, no queue.

    resolvePending()
    find looks that are due
claim the row  (no double-resolve)
fetch hourly rain for the window
missing hour? retry, up to 5 times
save outcome, update scorecard
    05

    The scorecard

    Honest scorecards.

    Every result shows the bar to beat beside the hit rate; too few looks get tally dots, not a percentage.

    3 of 10 looks logged

    The promise

    Never a verdict the data can’t back.

    • On the trailStill collecting looks
    • PromisingBeats chance
    • Same as chanceA real finding
    • BackfiresWorse than chance

    Under 30 sign-present days (backtest) or 10 looks (field), you get tally dots instead of a percentage. Backtest and field results are never pooled.
