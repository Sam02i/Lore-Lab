THE OLD WAY

How closed corporate AI and modern weather apps trap you behind glass with agreeable hallucinations

Sycophantic Chat Confirmation Bias

Ask a closed frontier model if cows lying down predicts rain, and it drafts a polite 400-word agreeable essay. No base rates, no confidence intervals, zero statistical rigor—just automated flattery masquerading as insight.

The 10-Day Radar Doomscroll

Commercial weather apps engineered like casinos—littered with animated radar loops, ad-bloated hourly forecasts, and notification spam designed to keep your eyes glued to glass instead of looking up at the horizon.

Cloud Surveillance for Family Lore

Sending your grandmother's regional dialect, generational oral history, and precise backyard coordinates over corporate API paywalls to subsidize closed training clusters.

Black-Box "Truth" Engines

Proprietary models that fake confidence. When an unmeasurable biological sign appears, closed assistants invent fake correlations instead of admitting: "There is no satellite dataset for swallows."

LORE LAB WAY

Open-weight intelligence, ruthless statistical honesty, and an app that tells you to put your phone away.

Brutal Statistical Honesty

We don't flatter ancient lore. We compute 10-year base rates, calculate Wilson score intervals, and measure true lift. If your grandmother's saying did no better than a coin flip, Lore Lab prints it plainly: "Same as chance. That's a real finding."

5-Second Field Observations

Look up at the swifts, tap Seen or Not seen, and step away. The interface explicitly instructs: "Logged. We'll check the sky for you. Put your phone away." The screen is an observation notebook, not a destination.

Local Gemma on Open Silicon

Run open-weight Gemma locally on your own machine. Multilingual proverbs—in German, Spanish, Hindi, or English—are interpreted on-device. Generational folk wisdom never leaves your local hardware.

Gemma Proposes, Code Decides

Zero LLM hallucinations in the science layer. Open weights extract hypotheses into strict Pydantic schemas, but deterministic, auditable Python code enforces the sensor whitelist. Honest code, open weights, real grass.
